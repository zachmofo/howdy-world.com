What is Howdy World?

Howdy World is a free resource where Texas youth — and anyone who works the land — learn real coding through projects that actually matter on a ranch, farm, or homestead.

First project: a Ranch Record Card. Five easy lessons that stack — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, JSON, and fetch — using the famous Fort Worth Stockyards longhorns as our example herd. No shopping carts. No fake to-do lists. Just useful tools with a Texas twist.

Garden sensors and ranch automations come later. We start with the bones.
