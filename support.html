Keep Howdy World free

Lessons stay free. Support is optional.

Why this page exists

Howdy World is a free coding resource for Texas youth and anyone who works the land. No paywall. No course upsell. If this work is useful, a little support covers the domain, the mic, and the time it takes to film the next lesson.

This is not a tax-deductible charity. It’s just a guy in Texas trying to keep the lights on for the next video. If you send something, put Howdy World in the note so I know where it came from.

Ways to help

The free option is still the best one: share a lesson with a kid who would actually use it.

Share it

Send the channel to a 4-H family, a homeschool group, or a shop teacher. That helps more than a dollar.
