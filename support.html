The learning side of Howdy World

Keep the learning side free.

Howdy World’s coding lessons are still here. Sharing a lesson—or making an optional contribution—helps support that free resource.

A little help goes a long way.

Howdy World is a free coding resource for Texas youth and anyone who works the land. No paywall. No course upsell. If this work is useful, a little support covers the domain, the mic, and the time it takes to film the next lesson.

The free option is still the best one: share a lesson with a kid who would actually use it. Send the channel to a 4-H family, a homeschool group, or a shop teacher.
